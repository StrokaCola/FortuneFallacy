A composable card system, not 1,100 paintings.

The artist delivers a small set of reusable layers; the engine composites them into ~1,100 unique card states. No per-catalyst paintings — system work first, optional bespoke portraits later, scoped separately. Anti-mystical voice carries from the boss brief: catalysts are instruments, not creatures. The shop is a workshop.

00 · shared style bible

Inherits the boss brief. Card-specific rules layer on top.

These are objects you could hold. A lens, a gauge, a calibration weight, a ferromagnetic shard, a sample under glass. If the artist's gut reach is "what creature personifies this mechanic" — that is the wrong gut.

Rule 01

Period reference

Scientific-instrument plates × 60s industrial design catalog × Voyager Golden Record. Think the panel diagrams from a 1970s spectrometer manual, redrawn with the gravity of a museum plaque. No mysticism, no occult symbology, no tarot, no robed figures, no spells.
Rule 02

Instruments, never creatures

Every catalyst is an instrument or artifact, never a creature or spell. A lens, a gauge, a coin press, a calibration weight, a phase diagram. The shop is a workshop, not a bestiary.
Rule 03

Readable at 64×88

Silhouette + frame + edition treatment must hold at strip size. If the player can't tell Common-Common from Legendary-Void at thumbnail, the system has failed. Test every layer at 64×88 before signing it off at 240×320.
Rule 04

Tint pass · 70 / 20 / 10

Each catalyst ships an identity color in CATALYST_META. The painted layers must accept it as a tint pass. The card is 70% catalyst-color anchored + 20% archetype band tint + 10% rarity/edition accent. Paint in neutral grayscale; the engine tints.
Rule 05

Layered, not flat

Every asset ships as a layered PSD with named groups — base, highlight, shadow, animatable-sweep — so the engine can isolate animated parts (foil sweep, poly rotation, void aurora bleed) without re-compositing the whole card.
Rule 06

Engraved, not flourished

Where ornament IS used — rarity frames, legendary cartouches — it should feel engraved on instrument metal. Brass-plate engraving, not calligraphy. Line economy carries over from the boss brief.
Rule 07

Aurora-violet underlight at the card edge

The same #9577ff rim glow from the boss brief carries here. Every card sits inside the same cosmos; the rim glow is the visual rhyme between the eight final-trial plates and the seventy-one catalysts that fight them. It's painted into the layer at the card boundary — the engine doesn't add it.

06 · icon style continuation

Sixty-seven shipped. Four missing. Five rules to keep them coherent.

Existing 67 SVG glyphs at public/brand/catalysts/ define the baseline. The KindFrame component sizes off these assumptions.

01
viewBox
0 0 24 24. No exceptions. The KindFrame component sizes from this assumption.
02
Stroke
Monoline, weight 1.5–2.0. No variable-weight strokes. No gradients.
03
Fills
Stroke-only by default. One small filled dot/shape allowed as emphasis. No glyph > ~30% filled area.
04
Complexity ceiling
Max 6 path elements per glyph. If a concept needs more, re-frame the concept.
05
Color
Paths use stroke="currentColor" and fill="currentColor" ONLY. Never embed a hex.

Four missing glyphs

Each ships as a stroke-only SVG following the 5 rules above. Filenames land in public/brand/catalysts/ as {catalyst_id}.svg.

09 · what this does NOT cover

Out of scope. Locked.

Handled elsewhere or by different roles. Listed explicitly so scope creep doesn't bleed.

animation timing
Engineer's job. The brief specifies where in the asset the animation hooks live (foil sweep, poly rotation, void aurora pulse, legendary fire pulse) but the artist does not deliver motion. The painted asset is the frame, not the film.
per-catalyst portraits at scale (71+)
Explicitly out. Only 5 legendary catalysts get bespoke portraits, and those are stretch deliverables priced separately. The other 66 inherit identity from (band × frame × edition × glyph). That's the whole point.
cosmetic-shop skin variants
Separate brief. The cosmetic system (Aurora Sigils palette, Solar Flare particles, Aurora Lyra constellation) handles palette swaps at the cosmetic layer. Do not paint cosmetic variants here.
mod cards
Separate brief. Crown · Resonance · Pip Charge · Loaded · Tally Mark etc. share the kind-frame system at the React level but ship their own archetype + rarity treatment after catalyst lands.
voucher · consumable · pack cards
Out. Different anatomy (no archetype band, no edition system) and ship their own pass.
catalyst icon redesigns
Only the 4 missing glyphs are in scope. The existing 67 are LOCKED. Do not propose stylistic revisions to glyphs that already shipped.
React-component frame redesigns
The engine's existing KindFrame outer hexagonal silhouette stays. The painted rarity frames live INSIDE that hexagonal stroke, not replacing it.
bespoke art for the boss roster
See Boss Roster Brief. Sibling document; different artist if portfolios don't overlap.

routing note

The shop is a workshop. The catalyst is a tool. The card is the tool's data sheet. If the artist's portfolio is fantasy character art or trading-card creature illustration, route to a different artist — this brief is intentionally hostile to anthropomorphic or mystical interpretation.
