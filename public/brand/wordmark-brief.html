NOTES
Letterforms are Cinzel 900 with +180 (square) / +220 (horizontal) tracking, hand-set to feel etched into a brass instrument plate. The aurora glow behind the wordmark is a soft Gaussian wash, not a drop shadow — read as backlight, not bevel. The 5-star polyline is a fabricated asterism (no real catalogued constellation) drawn in chart-style: bone-white discs at measured radii, gold hairlines between. The gold hairline beneath the wordmark is 1.5px at native resolution. Transparent variants strip the cosmos field and the aurora wash so the lockup composites onto any backdrop — the brief specifies transparent PNG, so the cosmos field is treated as a recommended backdrop, not part of the mark.
